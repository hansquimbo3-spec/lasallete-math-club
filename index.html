Welcome

Are you a student who loves solving complex equations, cracking logic puzzles, or exploring advanced math concepts beyond the classroom? The Lasallete Math Club is the perfect community for you!

Our club is designed for math wizards who want to connect with like-minded peers, sharpen their skills, and compete in local and national math competitions. This website will keep you updated on all our activities and help you register to become a member.

Register as a Member

To join the Lasallete Math Club, you must:

  • Be a student of Lasallete Highschool (Grades 7–12)
  • Have a strong interest and aptitude in mathematics
  • Commit to attending regular meetings and events

Fill out the registration form available at the Math Department Office, or talk to Ms. Mendez or Mr. Santos to sign up.
